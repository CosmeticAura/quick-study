Arctic simulation: the quick read

You are the President (with Kwesi). About 3 minutes.

1Your position

Trade plus security, with safeguards.

Priorities: economic development and national security. Motto: "Nothing ventured, nothing gained."

Option 1 (Northwest Passage trade) + Option 2 (security), with Option 3 (research, sustainability, Indigenous protection) built in. Listen to everyone, but don't change just to make compromise easier. Change only if the evidence is convincing.

2Your opening, in four beats

  • Position. I back trade and security.
  • Why. The ice is changing, so there are new opportunities.
  • Honest limit. Shipping is still dangerous and expensive.
  • Plan. Icebreakers, ports, communications and rescue. Work with Canada. Protect local communities. I'll hear your evidence before deciding what gets funded first.

3Four facts to remember

28%
Summer sea ice in 2025 was 28% smaller than in 2005.NOAA Arctic Report Card, Meier et al.
38
Full Northwest Passage trips in 2024. Not all were regular cargo.Justin Barnes, Belfer Center
Ice
Ice choke points shorten the shipping season.Cook et al., 2024
Law
Canada: internal waters. US: international strait.Barnes; Canada's Arctic Foreign Policy

Also: the Northwest Passage runs through Canada's islands, the Northern Sea Route along Russia's coast. Shorter does not always mean cheaper.

4The three options

1. Trade and development+ Shorter routes, growth, jobs, infrastructure− Costly, dangerous ice, unsure demand, dispute with Canada, environment
2. Security+ Readiness, protects allies, surveillance− High cost, escalation risk, harsh conditions
3. Science, sustainability, communities+ Research, ice forecasts, environment, local voice, diplomacy− Slow payoff, weak on security alone

5Who you ask, and what

TreasuryWhat will it cost? Is there enough demand?
DefenseWhich threats are confirmed? How do we respond without raising tensions?
NOAA, Coast GuardHow dangerous is the ice? What rescue and navigation do we need?
CanadaHow do we cooperate despite the legal dispute?
Inuit, AlaskaWhat protections and local benefits should come with it?
CongressWhich projects get funded, and what evidence justifies it?

6When someone pushes back

  • "I agree it's risky, so safety comes first: icebreakers, communications, rescue."
  • "I'll sequence the projects, but I'm not dropping the goal."
  • "Is that a research finding or an opinion?"
  • "Development only goes forward with consultation and protections for Inuit communities."

Don't say

  • That all 38 trips were commercial shipping.
  • An exact number of active Russian bases. Say Russia has increased activity and reopened former Soviet sites.
  • That a shorter route is automatically cheaper.
